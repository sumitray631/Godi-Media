About GODI MEDIA TV NEWS

We are India's most fearless (and sometimes fearless-ly fictional) news network. Our motto: "हम केवल झूठ बोलेंगे" — because the truth is overrated.

— Established 2026, headquartered in New Delhi.

Who We Are

GODI MEDIA TV NEWS is a bold, unapologetic news platform that delivers stories with a twist. We blend journalism with entertainment, and sometimes we bend the facts — but always with a wink.

Our team of dedicated reporters, anchors, and digital storytellers work 24/7 to bring you the most interesting version of the news. Whether it's politics, sports, or Bollywood gossip, we cover it all with a distinctive orange-and-black flair.

📢 Our Promise: We will never let the truth get in the way of a good story. But hey, you're smart — you know what's real.

With a growing audience across India and the world, GODI MEDIA has become a household name for those who like their news with a side of satire.
